# Receipts and Reports

## Receipt
A receipt should contain:
- School name/logo
- Receipt/transaction number
- Student
- Student number
- Date
- Payment breakdown
- Total amount
- Payment method
- Cashier
- Remarks if applicable

## Registrar Reports
- Enrollment list
- Applicants by status
- Requirements status
- Students by program/year/section
- Enrollment history

## Teacher Reports
- Class list
- Attendance
- Grades

## Cashier Reports
- Daily collections
- Monthly collections
- Payment history
- Outstanding balances
- Student account statement

## Export
Where appropriate, reports should support PDF and spreadsheet-compatible export.
