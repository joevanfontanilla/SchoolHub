# Objectives

## General Objective
To design and develop a web-based School Management and Enrollment System that centralizes student registration, enrollment processing, status tracking, academic records, fee management, payments, announcements, notifications, and administrative operations.

## Specific Objectives
1. Provide secure student account registration and authentication.
2. Allow students to submit enrollment applications online.
3. Allow students to upload and monitor required documents.
4. Allow registrars to review and process enrollment applications.
5. Provide transparent enrollment status tracking.
6. Manage student records and enrollment history.
7. Manage programs, subjects, sections, and class assignments.
8. Allow teachers to record attendance and grades.
9. Manage student fees, balances, payments, and receipts.
10. Provide school-wide and role-targeted announcements.
11. Provide notifications for important account and workflow events.
12. Generate administrative, academic, enrollment, and financial reports.
13. Maintain audit trails for sensitive actions.
14. Provide secure backup and recovery mechanisms.
15. Provide centralized system administration through the Super Admin role.
