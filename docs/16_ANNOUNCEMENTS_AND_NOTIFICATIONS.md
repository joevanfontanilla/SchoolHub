# Announcements and Notifications

## Announcements
Fields:
- Title
- Content
- Category
- Image (optional)
- Target audience
- Publish date
- Expiration date
- Status
- Author

## Target Audiences
- Everyone
- Students
- Teachers
- Registrar
- Cashier
- Specific program
- Specific year level

## Notifications
Trigger examples:
- Enrollment submitted
- Requirement rejected
- Correction requested
- Enrollment approved
- Fee assessment generated
- Payment recorded
- Enrollment confirmed
- New announcement
- Password/security event

## Notification States
- UNREAD
- READ
- ARCHIVED

## Principle
Notifications should inform users without exposing information to users who are not authorized to see it.
